Step 2: how the translations scored

WMT24 English to Mexican Spanish. 9,330 translated passages: 622 English passages, each translated by 15 systems. Critical errors weigh 5 penalty points, matching the official scoring.

The three headline numbers

These three are about all 9,330 passages pooled together. They mix all 15 systems, so they describe the whole dataset, not any one system.

Perfect (0 penalty points)77%About 7,180 passages had no error marked at all.
At least one major error9%About 860 passages had at least one serious mistake.
Average penalty points per passage0.99All penalty points added up, divided by 9,330.

Why the average is about 1 when most passages score 0

A few passages carry a lot of penalty points and pull the average up. The typical passage scores 0. The bars below count the same 9,330 passages two ways.

The 9% of passages with a major error hold 83% of all penalty points. One broken system, MSLC, holds 46% on its own. Leave MSLC out and the average drops to 0.58.

Which systems are best?

Average penalty points per passage for each system, across its 622 passages. Shorter is better. GPT-4's 0.12 means most of its passages scored 0. Blue is refA, the human translator.

refA's place is mostly a quirk. On the speech passages, reviewers compared it to an automatic transcript, not to what was actually said. Leave speech out and refA ranks 4th of 15.
